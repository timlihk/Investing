## ⚠️ Verification required before sizing

**IHI Aerospace's SRM position is industry knowledge, not source-confirmed in this build.** It was not established by any document pulled during construction of this theme. Before taking a position:

1. Pull IHI's segment disclosure and locate Aerospace/Defense revenue and margin
2. Confirm the SRM product line and its share of that segment
3. Check the Japanese standoff-missile programme allocation flowing to IHI specifically

If the SRM business turns out to be immaterial to consolidated earnings, this becomes a NEUTRAL on the same logic that caps NewMarket at 76 — the chokepoint can be real and still not move the P&L. **The score assumes materiality that has not been demonstrated.** This is flagged in `criteria.md` and on the dashboard row.

## Why Japan is structurally different from Europe and Korea

Japan in this book is not a rearmament-spending story — it is a **physical input supplier to the United States**. That distinction is the whole international thesis:

- European and Korean defence de-rated on peace-deal expectations and war-consumption risk
- Japanese titanium sponge, carbon fibre and licensed interceptor production are driven by **allied structural supply**, which survives a ceasefire

IHI sits on the supply side of that line.

## Risks

- **Materiality unverified** (above) — the single largest risk to this specific idea
- **JPY exposure** against a USD-funded book
- **Conglomerate structure** — civil aero engines dominate the narrative and the earnings
- **Japanese defence budget politics** — the programme ramp is real but is a national appropriations process with its own timing risk
