## The idea

The forging bottleneck at a price the US names stopped offering in January.

`breakage 4 · alternatives 3 · qualification 4 · cogs 3 · capacity 4`

Large forgings are a genuinely narrow layer — the qualified heavy-forging houses in the allied world number in the handful, the capex cycle for a new press runs years, and qualification is programme-by-programme. JSW combines that with an ordnance business (artillery, gun systems) that puts it directly in the munitions chain rather than adjacent to it.

## Risks

- **No consensus forward P/E returned** by the terminal — the valuation leg rests on the target gap and the rec, not on a multiple. Pull the segment model before sizing.
- **Conglomerate dilution** — JSW spans industrial machinery and plastics equipment as well as ordnance and forgings. Same caveat that caps NewMarket.
- **JPY** against a USD-funded thesis.
- **Domestic-programme dependence** — the ordnance business is largely tied to Japanese appropriations rather than allied export demand.
- **Consensus can be wrong.** A +54% gap on a stock that has gone nowhere for a year sometimes means the sell-side has not marked its targets to the tape.
