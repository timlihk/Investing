## The position

The other half of the US solid rocket motor duopoly, and the cheaper one. Northrop has invested **>$1bn in SRM propulsion manufacturing since 2018**, and is currently building a **$178m modular energetics facility at Allegany Ballistics Laboratory (West Virginia) under a NAVSEA contract that doubles SRM capacity by autumn 2026.**

`breakage 5 · alternatives 4 · qualification 5 · cogs 3 · capacity 5` — identical inputs to LHX; this is one duopoly scored consistently.

## Q2 2026: record backlog, stock down

Northrop reported **record $105bn backlog** and raised full-year guidance. Adjusted EPS of **$7.68 beat the $6.82 consensus**. Shares fell ~2.5%. The Sentinel restructuring after Nunn-McCurdy added **$7.6bn to backlog** in the quarter (EMD contract now $13.3bn).

The stock sits well below its $774 52-week high on capital intensity and near-term margin compression — the same pattern as L3Harris, which is the organising observation of this entire book.

## The hair, stated plainly

**B-21 has ~$2.0bn of cumulative LRIP losses recognised, with $1.0bn of loss accrual remaining in other current liabilities.** A 2026 agreement to expand production capacity is intended to improve returns, but earnings stay sensitive to cost and execution assumptions.

This is real, it is large, and it has **nothing to do with the SRM thesis**. That is the trade-off: you get the same chokepoint as LHX at 18.6x instead of 22.0x, and you accept fixed-price bomber risk to get it. Sizing should reflect that the discount is compensation, not a free lunch.

## Why hold both NOC and LHX

They are not duplicate positions in the way two semis would be. The duopoly is the asset; owning one exposes you to single-company execution risk on a thesis that is structural. LHX carries IPO-catalyst upside and a higher multiple; NOC carries B-21 downside and a lower one. The blend expresses the chokepoint rather than either management team.

## Risks

- **B-21** cost growth beyond the current accrual.
- **Capex de-rating persists** — same as LHX.
- **New SRM entrants** qualifying at volume.
- **Upstream AP dependency** — see `NEUUS.md`.
