## The position

NewMarket owns **AMPAC (American Pacific Corporation)** — the **sole large-scale US producer of ammonium perchlorate**, the oxidiser in every solid rocket motor. Cedar City, Utah. Approximately 170 full-time employees.

Perfect 5s across all five questions:

`breakage 5 · alternatives 5 · qualification 5 · cogs 5 · capacity 5`

- **breakage** — no AP, no solid rockets. Not "delayed": stopped.
- **alternatives** — sole large-scale domestic supplier. Customers contract long-term rather than spot precisely because there is no spot.
- **qualification** — energetics qualification is measured in years and gated by permits.
- **cogs** — the textbook cheap-but-critical position. AP is a small share of a missile's cost and 100% of its ability to leave the tube.
- **capacity** — a single site, environmental permitting, specialist workforce.

**Catalyst:** board approved **$100m** to expand AMPAC's AP capacity by **>50%**, explicitly to meet demand from solid rocket motors for defence and space launch.

## The honest problem: dilution

This is why the score is 76 and not 90. AMPAC is ~170 employees inside a company whose earnings come from **petroleum additives**. The chokepoint is genuine and the capacity expansion is real, but a >50% increase on a small base will not transform the consolidated P&L.

**Before sizing this, quantify the segment.** If AMPAC is low-single-digit percent of EBIT, this is a thematically satisfying position that will not move with the theme. Per `criteria.md` rule 4, corporate dilution caps Growth and Catalyst points even where the chokepoint points are fully earned.

## Why it is here anyway

Two reasons. First, it is the **only** listed way to own the AP chokepoint — Eurenco is private, GD-OTS is buried inside GD, and the new entrants are venture-backed. Second, it sets the reference point for the whole map: if you want to understand why $6bn did not fix 155mm output, start with the fact that one 170-person site gates every solid rocket motor in the country.

## Risks

- **Dilution** (above) is the primary one — the thesis can be right and the stock can not care.
- **Petroleum additives cyclicality** drives the actual earnings.
- **No sell-side anchor** — the terminal returned neither a forward P/E nor a target, so there is no consensus to disagree with.
- **Substitution research.** Alternative oxidisers and reduced-signature formulations are an active area; a genuine AP replacement would be a decade out but would be terminal.
