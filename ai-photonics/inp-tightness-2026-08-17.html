One-sentence take

InP wafers and the lasers grown on them remain the narrowest physical bottleneck in AI optics; the new 經濟日報 piece is a Taiwan-language amplification of a constraint LITE, COHR and Citi already described. What changed today is the claimed step-up in hike size (from 3–5% to >10%) and the explicit naming of the three Taiwan merchant epi houses. What did not change is the valuation: 3081 at 84x TTM sales / 138x FY27E EPS is no longer a discovery name.

Treat the 10% figure as an industry-source channel check (記者陳昱翔 quoting 業界人士), not a posted price list. The independently verified facts sit one layer down: long-term substrate lock-ups, COHR “primary constraint,” and Citi’s three-year EML deficit.

Stack — what actually has to scale

indium / red phosphorus → InP substrate (AXTI · JX 5016 · Sumitomo 5802) → merchant epi (VPEC 2455 · LandMark 3081 · IET 4971 · IQE) → EML / CW / PD die (LITE · COHR · SEI · WIN 3105) → 800G / 1.6T module or ELS / CPO engine → GPU / switch rack

SiPh does not retire this stack. Silicon cannot emit at 1310/1550; every SiPh PIC still needs a hybrid or external InP laser. Citi (16 Jul) is explicit: InP demand climbs with AI bandwidth “regardless of how much ground silicon photonics gains for integration.”

If AI optical demand doubles, the first thing that breaks is qualified InP wafers and the high-power CW / EML die grown on them — not module assembly, not SOI, not DSP. That is unchanged from the June bottleneck note. The new information is that merchant epi in Taiwan is now being offered as the liquid expression of that tightness, after LITE/COHR already locked substrate.

How to read the three Taiwan epi names

聯亞 LandMark 3081 — the priced-for-perfection expression

  • TTM sales NT$2.20bn, NI NT$428m, GM 42.7%, OM 23.1%. Company-reported Q2 sales NT$1.221bn / H1 NT$2.125bn (+111% YoY). Bloomberg TTM 2.20bn is the trailing four-quarter print and lags the H1 run-rate.
  • Street FY27E sales NT$5.34bn / EPS 19.35; FY28E NT$10.47bn / EPS 41.70. Four-week revisions still chasing: FY27 sales +387m, FY28 +1,093m.
  • Even on FY28E the stock is ~64x earnings and ~26x sales. MS moved OW → EW (Mar) then held EW at TP 2,850 after 1Q (Apr) because “the stock had already discounted the good news.” Price is now 2,670 vs that 2,850 and vs consensus 3,134 — the residual is execution of the 2.5× 2027 capacity plan, not discovery.
  • China was 73% of 2024 sales. That is a geopolitical and customer-concentration fact, not a footnote.

全新 VPEC 2455 — current-production epi, mixed book

  • TTM sales NT$3.38bn, NI NT$548m, GM 36.1%, OM 19.8%. Larger and more profitable today than LandMark, cheaper on every multiple, but the book is still RF/HBT + optical, not a SiPh pure-play.
  • Jul monthly NT$422m is the first hard confirmation that 3Q is inflecting the way the article claims. Street FY27E EPS 5.37 (4wk −0.09) — revisions are not chasing the way they are on 3081.
  • Price 417 vs consensus TP 430. Fourteen analysts, 4.79 rec. The easy multiple expansion versus TP is gone.

IET-KY 4971 — smaller, cheaper, US-fab option

  • TTM sales NT$1.08bn, NI NT$65m, GM 35.7%. FY27E EPS 8.03 → 62x; TP 746 implies +49% if Street is right, but only five analysts and rec 3.60 (hold-ish).
  • 3M −29% after the spring spike (it printed NT$959 in April). The US Texas phase-2 is 2026-end / 2027-start capacity, not 2026 earnings.
  • The APD / PIN breadcrumb to LITE and COHR is still trade-press plus company “多家知名大廠” language. Treat as mapped supplier candidate, not proven sole-source.

Substrate layer — this is still the actual choke

  • AXTI — TTM sales only $88m and still TTM-unprofitable (OM −25%), but Q2 InP print was the inflection: 华泰 31 Jul said InP sales >$30m, GM 45%, non-GAAP OP $11.2m, backlog >$100m, YE26 quarterly InP capacity ~$60m → YE27 ~$130m. Street FY27E sales $218m (+$75m in four weeks) and FY28E $459m. The stock is a China-permit + Tongmei story wearing an InP multiple. AXTI remains the fragile choke, not a clean one.
  • JX 5016 — Nomura 8 Jul to Buy, TP ¥4,800, talking 7–10× InP capacity / mid-term 20× volume. JPM APAC (11 Aug, via X) said some investors want 20–30× by FY29 versus management 7–10×. A 11 Aug desk note flagged red-phosphorus / indium constraints and ~50% utilisation — treat the 20× slide as aspiration until wafers ship.
  • Sumitomo 5802 — Citi 2 Jul: 3.1× InP substrate capacity over FY25–29, ¥18bn. Citi 16 Jul puts SEI at ~31–60% of InP wafers depending on the exhibit, and as the only name that is both a wafer supplier and a finished EML/CW maker. LandMark’s five-year LTA is the first hard customer-side confirmation in our file. Optical is still a minority of a ¥7.5T auto/industrial conglomerate (P/E 18x, P/S 1.4x).
Share-of-market numbers for InP wafers are not internally consistent across brokers (AXT 19–70%, SEI 31–60%, JX “about 40% with SEI”). Use the qualitative fact — three names, China-license risk on one of them — and do not underwrite a precise share.

What would break this

  1. Q4 hike does not stick — module makers refuse the >10% and GM at 3081/2455 rolls over in 4Q/1Q prints.
  2. AXTI US export licenses flood, or Tongmei HK listing / China-domestic mix makes Western lock-ups less scarce than the tape thinks.
  3. SEI + JX wafers actually show up in 2027 at the high end of the 3–10× slides, and UHP CW / EML prices roll.
  4. CPO / NPO scale-up slips past 27H2 and 1.6T pluggable demand cools. (CPO delay alone is not enough — pluggables still eat InP.)
  5. LandMark 2027 capacity 2.5× misses, or China 73% mix becomes a policy problem.
